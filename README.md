### 📊 About Me

I come from a **B.Com (Commerce)** background, not a coding or computer science one. During my degree, I got curious about how businesses actually make decisions — and realized that behind every good decision is *data*. That curiosity led me to a Data & Business Analytics training program, where I'm now learning to turn raw numbers into insights that businesses can act on.

I'm not here to write complex software — I'm here to understand data, find patterns, and tell the story behind the numbers using tools built for exactly that. Economics gave me the "why," and analytics is giving me the "how."
